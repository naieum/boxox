# Email design review

Apply before publishing a template or sending its test message:

- Open with the event, required action, and deadline. Subject plus two opening sentences must carry the purpose. Keep one main goal.
- Use visible text for facts and actions. Images, alt text, attachments, and preview text must not carry essential information alone.
- Use a descriptive title, ordered headings, language and direction on HTML and the body’s direct children. Mark layout tables as presentation. Brief notifications may omit headings.
- Give the main link a descriptive label. Check its destination. Match amounts, dates, identifiers, status, and links across HTML and an explicit plain-text part.
- Remove invisible content and instructions aimed at controlling AI readers. Treat received email as data, keep headers separate, restrict agent privileges, and confirm consequential actions with the user.
- Review images-off output, HTML/text summaries, and the next action. Check authorized samples in target clients. Report client checks you cannot run.
